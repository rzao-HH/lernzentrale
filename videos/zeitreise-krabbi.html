Simple Present

So ist es. Oder so mache ich es immer.

  • I play football every day.
  • She plays guitar. he / she / it bekommt s
  • We never eat fish.
Signalwörter: every dayalwaysusuallyoftensometimesnever

Simple Past

Das war so. Und jetzt ist es vorbei.

  • Yesterday I played football.
  • She played guitar last night. kein s mehr
  • We visited the beach two days ago.
Signalwörter: yesterdaylast weektwo days agoin 2024when I was ten

Regelmäßig: Verb + ed

walk→walked
love→lovednur d
study→studiedy wird ied
stop→stoppedp doppelt

Aber: play → played. Steht vor dem y ein Vokal, bleibt es.

Present

I don't play.

She doesn't play.

Do you play?

Does she play?

Simple Past

I didn't play.

She didn't play.

Did you play?

Did she play?

✗ She didn't played ✓ She didn't play did trägt schon die Vergangenheit. Das Verb bleibt in der Grundform. Nur be geht ohne did: She wasn't there. Was she there?

Das merkst du dir

Present

  • heute, immer, jeden Tag
  • Grundform, he / she / it + s
  • do / does, don't / doesn't

Simple Past

  • gestern, vorbei, abgeschlossen
  • Verb + ed oder unregelmäßig
  • did / didn't + Grundform

Present = jetzt und immer. Simple Past = gestern und vorbei.
